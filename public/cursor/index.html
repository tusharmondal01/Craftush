A QUICK WALKTHROUGH

Three steps. One good video.

  1. Start.

    Choose Record screen, or Upload footage for an existing video. Recording options contains microphone and audio settings. Finish recording when your take is ready.

  2. Edit.

    Uploaded mobile recordings are scanned for visible touch dots. The camera zooms and pans along the detected path. Press F to add a full-frame moment; following resumes at the next touch gesture. The full motion timeline contains scale, horizontal pan and vertical pan. Drag keys to change their timing, or select one to edit its values. Missed dots can be set from Advanced motion.

  3. Export.

    Choose Continue to export, then Export video. Keep this tab visible until the download completes. Your original audio is preserved.

Just exploring? Try a demo to see an eight-second recording with automatic cursor tracking and zooms.
Tracking a cursor on another website

Download and unzip the optional extension from Cursor & tracking options in Edit. Open your browser’s extensions page, enable Developer mode, and choose Load unpacked. Before recording, choose Browser tab in Recording options. Then open the extension in your shared tab and choose Track this tab.

No account needed. Recordings and exports are processed on your device.
