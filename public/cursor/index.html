A QUICK WALKTHROUGH

From screen to scene.

  1. Choose what to capture.

    Press Record screen and select a screen, window, or browser tab in your browser’s sharing prompt. Enable your microphone if you want to narrate.

  2. Give your cursor a spotlight.

    For tracking on other websites, download and unzip the optional extension. Open your browser’s extensions page, enable Developer mode, and choose Load unpacked to select the unzipped folder. Start a recording with Browser tab selected, then click the extension icon in that shared tab and choose Track this tab.

  3. Reframe a phone recording.

    Choose Upload footage or drop a video into the preview. Use a compatible MP4, MOV, or WebM file; its original audio is kept. Press Z to zoom in or out, hold D while dragging the preview to pan, and click a detail to focus. Use Space to play or pause, or place a focus point at an exact moment. Click either shortcut button to choose your own zoom and drag keys. Adjust move duration and motion feel for smooth transitions.

  4. Finish, style, and share.

    Finish your recording, preview it, and adjust motion, background, corners, and canvas ratio. Export video saves a WebM video to your device.

Just exploring? Try a demo to see an eight-second recording with automatic cursor tracking and zooms.

No account needed. Recordings and exports are processed on your device.
